# Kevin’s Cookie Shop Tycoon

A small, self-contained browser game. No install, server, or internet connection is required.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Start with $100 and run the cookie shop for 30 days. Each morning, choose how many cookies to bake, their price, and your advertising budget. Close the shop to see sales, revenue, expenses, profit, and remaining cash, then plan the next day.

Ingredients cost $0.50 per cookie, advertising is paid up front, and unsold cookies are wasted. Each day’s neighborhood brings different demand. Keep an eye on your cash before committing to a batch.
